SmartHit Tennis App Privacy Policy

Current Version: 1.1.0

Updated: October 2026

Effective: October 2026

SmartHit Tennis App (hereinafter referred to as "we", "us", or "SmartHit") values your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information.

⚠️ Important Notice

Please read this privacy policy carefully and ensure you fully understand and agree before using our services.

【Special Statement】

This application adheres to the following privacy protection principles and regulations:

  • Compliance with the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China
  • Compliance with the Cybersecurity Law of the People's Republic of China
  • Compliance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework
  • Compliance with Apple's Privacy Manifest requirements (iOS 17+)
  • We do not track you across applications or websites
  • We do not use your personal information for advertising purposes

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

To provide our services, we need to collect the following information:

Phone Number (if you choose phone number registration)

  • Purpose: Account registration, identity verification, security protection
  • Storage: Encrypted storage on Tencent CloudBase database
  • Required: No (you can choose other login methods)

WeChat Login Information (if you choose WeChat login)

  • Data Collected: WeChat OpenID, nickname, avatar (if authorized)
  • Purpose: Account creation, identity recognition
  • Storage: Encrypted storage on Tencent CloudBase database
  • Required: No (you can choose other login methods)

Apple ID Information (if you choose Apple ID login)

  • Data Collected: Apple user identifier, email (if authorized)
  • Purpose: Account creation, identity verification
  • Storage: Encrypted storage on Tencent CloudBase database
  • Required: No (you can choose other login methods)

1.2 Personal Profile (Optional)

You may optionally provide the following information for a better experience:

  • Basic Information: Nickname, gender, avatar
  • Tennis Information: Dominant hand, tennis level, personal bio
  • Purpose: Personalized service experience, training data analysis
  • Required: No, you may choose not to provide this information

1.3 Device and Usage Information

To optimize performance and improve our services, we automatically collect:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, device identifier
  • Usage Statistics: Feature usage frequency, video processing duration, session records, and actions in the app (such as favoriting a rally, choosing which player is you, opening the membership page)
  • Usage Before Sign-in: Linked to a random anonymous device ID generated by the app (not the advertising identifier; it is reset when the app is uninstalled); after you sign in, it is linked to your account
  • Diagnostic Information: Crash logs, performance data, error reports
  • Ad Attribution: If you downloaded the app through an Apple Search Ads ad, we receive attribution information from Apple's AdServices (campaign, ad group, keyword, country or region) and associate it with your account. This does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not require tracking permission
  • Purpose: Optimize app performance, improve user experience, resolve technical issues, calculate membership quotas
  • Processing: Usage statistics are linked to your account and stored on our servers; they are not used for advertising and are not sold or provided to third parties

1.4 Play Records (when you use Records)

  • What We Collect: Play date, start time and duration of each session, mood of the day, your note for each session (up to 200 characters), monthly goals; for sessions from a video, also that session's statistics (shots, rallies, longest rally, serve speed, in / out landings) — never any video footage
  • Purpose: Saving and showing your play records and monthly statistics, and restoring them after you change phones or reinstall the app
  • Storage: Stored in Tencent CloudBase, linked to your account
  • Required: No — nothing is created unless you use Records

1.5 Health Data and Voice Input (separate permission required)

  • Apple Health: With your permission, we read heart rate and "Tennis" workouts (start time, duration) to create play records automatically and show your heart rate during play. We only read and never write to Health. Heart rate is used only on your device and is never uploaded; play records created from workouts (start time, duration) are saved as described in 1.4
  • Voice Input: When you press and hold to speak in Records, the built-in iOS speech recognition turns your words into text for your note. When the device supports it, recognition happens on the device only and no audio is uploaded; otherwise it is processed by Apple's speech recognition service (Apple's privacy policy applies). We never receive or store recordings
  • Turning It Off: You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings - Health - Data Access & Devices, and Settings - Privacy & Security - Speech Recognition

1.6 Subscription and Payment Information (when you buy a membership)

  • Payment: Payments are handled by Apple; we never receive your bank card or other payment details
  • What We Collect: Transaction ID, purchased product, price and currency, App Store country or region, subscription expiry date
  • Purpose: Confirming your membership benefits, restoring purchases, handling support requests
  • Storage: Stored in Tencent CloudBase, linked to your account

1.7 Push Notifications (permission required)

  • How We Use It: With your consent, we send service notifications and activity reminders through JPush
  • What We Collect: A push registration ID (linked to your account); to deliver notifications, the JPush SDK collects device information such as device model, system version and network status
  • Turning It Off: You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings - Notifications

1.8 Information We Explicitly Do Not Collect

  • ❌ Your training video content
  • ❌ Your geographic location
  • ❌ Your contacts or address book
  • ❌ Your usage data from other applications
  • ❌ Your heart rate or other health data (used only on your device, never uploaded to our servers)

2. Purpose of Information Use

We collect and use your personal information solely for the following legitimate purposes:

2.1 Providing and Improving Services

  • Creating and managing your account
  • Providing video clipping and editing features
  • Saving your training records and preferences
  • Optimizing AI model performance and accuracy
  • Improving application features and user experience
  • Understanding which promotional channel (such as Apple Search Ads) led you to download the app, to evaluate the effectiveness of our promotion
  • Creating your play records, monthly statistics and goal progress
  • Confirming your membership benefits and quotas

2.2 Security and Prevention

  • Verifying user identity and preventing fraud
  • Detecting and preventing security threats
  • Ensuring the safe and stable operation of our services
  • Responding to legal requirements and law enforcement requests

2.3 Communication and Support

  • Responding to your inquiries and feedback
  • Sending important service notifications and updates
  • Providing technical support and customer service
  • Sending push notifications with your consent (you can turn them off at any time)

2.4 We Will Not Use Your Information For

  • ❌ Showing you personalized or third-party advertising
  • ❌ Selling or renting to third parties
  • ❌ Cross-app or cross-website tracking
  • ❌ Any purpose unrelated to our services

3. Local Data Processing (Core Feature)

3.1 Local Processing of Video Data

This is our most important privacy protection measure:

  • Storage Location: All training videos are stored entirely on your local device
  • Processing Method: AI recognition and clipping is performed using your device's GPU
  • Privacy Guarantee: Video content is never uploaded to any server
  • User Control: You can delete local video files at any time

3.2 Processing Results

  • Local Storage: Rally recognition results, player identification, shot and landing analysis are stored on your device
  • Cloud Storage: After you sign in, play records (see 1.4), usage statistics and membership usage are saved to the cloud — never any video footage
  • Your Control: Delete a day's record in Records, or delete your account to remove all cloud data

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 We Do Not Actively Share Your Personal Information

We will not share your personal information with third parties except in the following circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent
  • As required by laws and regulations
  • As required by judicial or governmental authorities
  • To protect our or others' legitimate rights and interests

4.2 Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, which may have access to some of your information:

Tencent Cloud SMS Service

  • Purpose: Sending phone verification codes
  • Shared Information: Phone number, verification code
  • Privacy Policy: https://cloud.tencent.com/document/product/301/11470

WeChat Open Platform

  • Purpose: Providing WeChat login functionality
  • Shared Information: WeChat authorization information (OpenID)
  • Privacy Policy: WeChat Privacy Policy

Tencent CloudBase

  • Purpose: Storing account information and user data
  • Shared Information: Account information, play records, usage statistics, subscription information
  • Privacy Policy: https://cloud.tencent.com/document/product/301/11470

Apple Sign In

  • Purpose: Providing Apple ID login functionality
  • Shared Information: Apple user identifier, email (if authorized)
  • Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Apple Ad Attribution (AdServices)

  • Purpose: Determining whether you downloaded the app through Apple Search Ads, to evaluate promotion effectiveness
  • Shared Information: An attribution token generated by your device (contains no personal identity information such as your name or phone number)
  • Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

JPush (push notification service)

  • Purpose: Sending push notifications
  • Shared Information: Push registration ID, device model, system version, network status
  • Privacy Policy: https://www.jiguang.cn/license/privacy

Apple Speech Recognition

  • Purpose: Turning press-and-hold voice input into text when on-device recognition is not available
  • Shared Information: Your speech audio (sent by iOS directly to Apple; we never receive it)
  • Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Apple App Store In-App Purchase

  • Purpose: Processing membership subscription payments and verifying purchases
  • Shared Information: Transaction information (transaction ID, purchased product)
  • Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

4.3 Third-Party Data Security

  • All third-party services have undergone security assessments
  • Only necessary authentication information is transmitted
  • Core video data is always processed locally and is never transmitted to any third party

5. Information Storage and Protection

5.1 Storage Location

  • Video Data: Stored entirely on your local device
  • Account Information: Stored on Tencent CloudBase servers located within China
  • Usage Statistics, Play Records, Subscription Information: Stored together with account information on Tencent CloudBase servers located within China

5.2 Retention Period

  • Account Information: Retained until you cancel your account
  • Usage Statistics: Automatically deleted after 12 months
  • Local Videos: Retained or deleted at your discretion

5.3 Security Measures

We implement the following security measures to protect your information:

  • Encrypted Transmission: Data transmitted using HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Encrypted Storage: Sensitive information is stored with encryption
  • Access Control: Strict employee access restrictions
  • Security Audits: Regular security assessments and audits
  • Local Processing: Video data never leaves your device, fundamentally eliminating leakage risks
