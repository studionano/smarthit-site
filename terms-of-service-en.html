1. The Service

1.1 What the App Does

SmartHit Tennis is an AI-powered app that automatically edits tennis videos. Its main features include:

  • Recording tennis practice and match videos
  • Automatically detecting rallies and cutting out downtime such as picking up balls
  • Smart filtering and management of rallies
  • Video editing with precise trimming and adjustments
  • Saving and managing your training records
  • Advanced features such as ball speed, bounce maps, trajectory effects and camera follow (some require a membership or SmartHit Beans; see Section 7)

1.2 Key Characteristics

  • On-device processing: all video processing happens on your device; original videos are never uploaded to the cloud
  • Real-time editing: videos can be processed while you record
  • AI-driven: computer vision automatically detects the ball and players
  • Privacy: your training videos stay under your control; we do not access or store your video content
